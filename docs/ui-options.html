Front-end look: three directions

Each option shows the main screen and the login page with sample numbers. All three keep the Sambhav teal and orange and the same layout. Only the visual style changes, so any of them can be built on what we already have. All three get a light and dark mode.

A · Steel Glass

Glassmorphism · light

Soft colour washes in Sambhav teal and orange behind frosted-glass panels. It's modern and premium, and the most "wow" of the three on a big screen. The risk is that too much glass can hurt readability on dense tables, so we keep glass on the frame and use plainer panels for tables. Font: Plus Jakarta Sans.

B · Control Room

Recommended

Dark-first, like a live operations screen. A faint grid, glowing teal numbers, and orange only for things that need attention. It's crisp and fast to read, ages well, and the dark mode is designed in from the start, not bolted on. The login has a slowly moving teal and orange glow behind the card. This is the style of Linear and Vercel. Font: Inter (or Geist), with the numbers in fixed-width figures so columns line up.

C · Industrial Editorial

Bold · light

Feels like a steel company: warm off-white paper, a dark steel sidebar, big confident numbers, and an orange rule on the key figures. The login shows a brushed-steel panel with one line of copy. It's the most "on brand" for a steel trader and very readable for senior users. Fonts: Manrope for headings and numbers, IBM Plex Sans for text.
