# AI mode (29 Oct 2026)

A quick answer panel in the search bar at the top of every page. Digital Brain stays the full workspace (history, files, saved questions); AI mode is for a short answer from wherever you are.

- **The search bar**: exact matches appear as you type (plates by heat / plate / certificate / lot number; for owner and administrators also Tally invoices and orders), free, no AI (`/api/search`). Enter on a plain search goes to Plates, as before. A text that looks like a question (ends with "?", starts with do/how/what/which/show..., or six words or more) highlights "Ask AI mode" first. The sparkle "AI mode" button or Ctrl+Enter always opens it. "/" or Ctrl+K jumps to the bar.
- **The panel** (right side on a computer, full screen on a phone): the answer streams in, numbered links to check each fact (same references as Digital Brain), follow-up questions, copy, thumbs up/down, **Think harder** (asks again with the stronger model, GPT-6.1 Terra; about 50 paise), and an arrow to continue in Digital Brain.
- **It knows the page**: the page name and the filters in the address are passed along ("this plate", "these invoices"), and the page narrows what the AI looks in (faster and cheaper). Each page has its own starting questions. A "go_to" lookup answers "take me to debtors" with a link; `paperwork_gaps` answers "which invoices have no PO / certificate".
- **History**: every AI mode conversation is stored as a Digital Brain chat (`ask_chats.origin = 'ai_mode'`, migration 38), marked "AI" in the Digital Brain list. The clock icon in the panel lists recent AI mode questions and searches inside past questions and answers; Digital Brain's chat search now also looks inside the words, not only titles.
- **Model**: Haiku (Fast) only, with 4 steps at most and answers of about 100 words (`mode: "quick"` in `/api/ask`, `lib/ask/engine.ts`). Measured cost: about 0.1 to 0.15 cent per question. The existing limits apply (40 questions per person per hour, $5 a day in all). Read-only, like Digital Brain.
- Code: `web/src/components/TopSearch.tsx` (bar and panel), `TopSearchGate.tsx` (who is signed in), `app/api/search/route.ts`, `app/api/ask/route.ts` (mode, page), `lib/ask/engine.ts` (`quickRules`), `lib/ask/tools.ts` (`go_to`, `paperwork_gaps`).
